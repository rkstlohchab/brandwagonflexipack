About Brandwagon

A practical packaging partner for everyday production.

Brandwagon Flexipack Private Limited brings together flexible film products, courier packaging and custom formats with a clear focus on dependable supply and application fit.

Our approach

Good packaging starts with a good specification.

Different products need different combinations of clarity, strength, sealing, friction, barrier performance, colour, thickness and format. The catalogue is structured so customers can get from an application to a practical product starting point quickly.

For custom programmes, the commercial team can work from dimensions, material requirements, artwork, target volumes and the packaging line or end-use environment.
